Hedgerow

Market
How to play
  • On your turn, pick one of the three market tiles and place it next to any tile on the board.
  • Then you may plant one of your 6 flags on the tile you just placed. That flag claims the whole connected patch of that land.
  • At the end, every patch is worth 1 point per hex. Whoever has more flags in it gets the points. Ties split them.
  • The game ends after 15 turns each. The scoreboard always shows the score as if the game ended right now.
  • Hover over a tile on the board to see its patch and who leads it.

Two players, one board

Take turns on this computer. Each turn: take a tile, place it, and decide whether to plant a flag.
